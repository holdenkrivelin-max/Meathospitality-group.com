How MEAT Points Work +

Too many great experiences are gated behind a paywall — reserved for whoever can already afford them. We don't think access should work that way. It shouldn't matter who you know or what you can spend. It should matter that you're willing to take the first step.

That's why we're building Meat Points — earned not just by spending, but by living. Getting up and out of the house. Talking to someone new. Starting the thing you've been putting off. Asking the question you've been too nervous to ask. Every step forward earns you points, and points unlock access — a seat at a dinner, entry into new tiers, experiences that used to be reserved for whoever could already afford them. The reward for growing is more access to grow.

Every verified check-in earns 10 Meat Points — and if you complete it with a meatie, that jumps to 20. Show up for your life, and the points add up fast.

Just Keep Meating reminders send a quick nudge in the morning to get you excited for your tasks that day — "Good morning. Just Keep Meating — here are your reminders for today."

Those points live inside MEAT Rewards — and it's not just for MEAT events and dinners. Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, coffee shops — Partner Events, Chef Features, and Chef Profiles for concepts still finding their footing — even ground-floor pre-order access to what's opening next. Why should you only get rewarded for doing one thing?

Pre-order from a Chef Profile and you'll see who else is waiting alongside you — building the hype before the place even opens its doors.

Gyms. Spas. Saunas. Day trips. Partner Events at the businesses you already love. The everyday things nobody else is rewarding you for. The bigger we grow, the more we'll add. Welcome to everyday living as it should be.

Here's how it works: do it on your own, or connect with a meatie who's doing something similar — complete the task, verify it happened, then move on to your next objective. No hollow self-checkboxes. Real steps, confirmed by real people.

We want to reward you for what you do every day, with things you already do. Went grocery shopping? That's life. Met a friend for coffee? Life. Went on a date, walked the dog, tried a new restaurant, worked on your idea, finally sent the email you've been avoiding? All life.

You don't necessarily have to do it alone. See what others are up to. Connect based on shared interest. Hold each other accountable. Complete the task together — or do it on your own. Verify each other's progress. Earn Meat Points. Keep moving. Either way, you earn points for taking that first step. We encourage you to go with how you feel and what you like — every activity is based on your interests. Someone should always be able to take the first step alone and still have it count — the connection is there for when you don't want to go it solo, not a requirement to earn access.

When you sign up, a short survey gets to know your interests — then we show you meaties who match your interests, so you can connect and share what you're up to or what you'd like to accomplish. Share, message, laugh, and connect. That's the Meat way.

Our goal is to create a MEAT Experience you'll be talking about for years to come. Once you sign up, you're part of the Meat family — for life.

We're not rewarding spending. We're rewarding participation in life. Go somewhere. Do something. Meat someone. Take the first step. MEAT rewards you for participating — and Meat Points turn participation into possibility.

Our Story

9
Years watching great concepts disappear

It was a day like any other. I had just come from a conversation with my mom and brother about my trajectory in life — the kind of conversation where you're expected to just accept things as they are. Walking out afterward, right across the street, I saw it: 30 Love, this big, beautiful, majestic bar and grill, closed, dark, a "for lease" sign in the window.

That was the moment. I was done accepting what life was supposed to be. There had to be a way for operators, foodies, and chefs to build the next big hospitality idea together — before it ended up behind a "for lease" sign of its own. I called it MEAT. By that night, my entire life had changed.

But 30 Love was only the spark. MEAT is really made up of a few pieces. A piece of my dad — not with me now, but the one who taught me to be relentless, to just keep showing up even when I didn't feel like it. A piece of Stormi, my dog, who taught me the same thing a different way: build trust over fear, one step at a time. And a piece of me — wanting to connect with somebody on a real level, and on top of that, wanting to do it with somebody else. To feel like I'm not alone.

I think that's why this resonates past just restaurants. It's not really about the industry. It's about showing up anyway.
