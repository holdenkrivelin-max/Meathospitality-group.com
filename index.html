Why MEAT Life

I'm Holden Krivelin, founder of MEAT Life.

MEAT Life was built to make life more human, more connected, and easier to move through.

Quality, comfort, access, and belonging should not be reserved for people behind a paywall. MEAT Hospitality Group builds products, experiences, and tools made for real life—so more people can take the first step.

Accessibility is the new luxury.

Technology should not make people feel limited. It should help people turn an idea into something real — giving it direction, support, community, and a place in the world.

MEAT Life is here to help you organize what matters, connect with people, discover what is next, and make every action count.
