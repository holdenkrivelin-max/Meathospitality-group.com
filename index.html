Mission
We build the things we wish existed.

MHG Group is a software company. That sentence took forty-three apps to earn: a planner for your time, a map that lives through people, a home for your moments, tools for your money, your music, your work, your people. Every one of them exists because it was missing — from our lives first, and then, it turned out, from everyone's.

Our method is the mission: name every idea so none are lost, start every concept so none are forgotten, and bring each one to life — one at a time, in the open, only when it's ready for prime time. We don't chase the room behind the velvet rope. We build the door, and we leave it open.

"We're a company built for the future, by the future."

Accessibility is the new luxury. Software is the building — and yes, sometimes we open a room in it.
