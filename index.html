Every Decision We Make

The MEAT Test.

Does this help someone participate more in their life?

If yes, it may belong. If it only increases screen time, data collection, or engagement without helping someone act, connect, create, or experience something in the real world — it probably doesn't. Every feature we build has to earn its place against that one question.

MEAT is not trying to become the place where you spend your life.

MEAT is trying to become the platform that helps you participate in the life you already have — and build the parts you still want.

We didn't invent showing up for each other, or trying something new, or building the thing you've been putting off. That's always been possible. We just built something new that's been right in front of us the whole time.

Before Anything Else

Trust & Safety.

The app doesn't exist yet — but the standard it's held to already does. These are the commitments the app is being built around from day one, not features bolted on after something goes wrong.

You control your MEAT experience.

Who you connect with, what you share, and when you participate are always your choice. MEAT is built for connection without pressure.

Who You Interact With

You choose who can reach you.

Friends-only, Circle-only, mutual connection, or open discovery — your call, not a default buried in settings.

No forced access.

Matching, joining the same Circle, or attending the same MEATUp never entitles anyone to your attention.

Verified before you can match.

Phone verification is the floor for creating a profile — no anonymous accounts, no bots pretending to be people.

What Control You Have

Profile privacy is your call.

Location precision, workplace, schedule, goals, financial progress, relationship status — control what's visible. MEAT should never make somebody expose personal information just to participate.

Message requests, held separately.

Requests sit in their own inbox until you accept them. Repeated unwanted contact gets restricted quickly, not after the tenth message.

Only what's needed, nothing more.

No workplace, no exact address, no background tracking. What we don't collect can't be a problem later.

Transparent, always.

You'll know what MEAT knows about you, why it uses that information, and have simple controls to change or delete it.

When Something Feels Wrong

Block and report, one tap away.

Reachable directly from any profile or conversation — obvious placement, no guilt, no confusing process.

A real person reviewing reports.

Every report gets looked at by a human, not a queue that quietly disappears. Safety concerns get a same-day response.

MEATUps built for safety, not just fun.

Clear host identity, location details, attendee expectations, and a simple code of conduct. First-time one-on-one meetups default to public places, with an easy way to share the details with someone you trust.

Circles with real rules.

Clear standards against harassment, sexual pressure, stalking, threats, degrading comments, and discrimination — enforced, not just posted somewhere.

A real bridge, not a replacement, for real help.

A meatie is a great accountability partner for a lot of things. For substance use, mental health, or recovery, MEAT will always point you toward real, trained support first — not a stranger from an app. SAMHSA's National Helpline (1-800-662-HELP) is free, confidential, and available 24/7.

Built with women, not just for them.

Design for everyone means women are represented in the photography, examples, leadership, testing group, and actual product decisions — without the brand becoming patronizing. Before this ships, women test the rough product and get asked directly: where would you hesitate, what feels invasive, what would make you leave? Their behavior teaches us more than guessing ever could.

Connection should never require surrendering your boundaries.

This list will only get more specific as the app gets built — not less.

What is MEAT Next?

Personalized guidance for what comes next — based on where you are, what you've done, and where you want to go.

MEAT Next is MEAT's personalized "what's next?" layer. After you finish what you already needed to do, MEAT Next helps you decide where to go from there based on your goals, interests, progress, routines, and what matters to you right now.

It can suggest the next step toward a goal, a follow-up from something you completed, an activity that fits your interests, someone to connect with, something you've been putting off, or a new direction when you're not sure what to do next.

Built from you.

Always about what's next.

Optional, always.

What is MEAT Count?

A visible record of forward movement.

MEAT Count is more than a step counter. It's every meaningful thing you did today, across every part of your life — 3 steps toward fitness, 2 steps toward starting a business, a bill paid, $50 put toward debt, $100 moved into savings, a subscription you finally canceled. All of it counts.

Then MEAT shows both sides: what you did today, and where it's actually taking you. MEAT Count Today: 7 — seven meaningful steps. Financial Goal: $1,250 of $5,000 saved, 25% complete. A lot of people do responsible little things every day and still feel like they're standing still, because those actions never get collected into one picture. MEAT Count makes the invisible progress visible.

Three layers, one picture.

Tasks — what needs to get done. MEAT Count — how many meaningful steps you took. Goal Progress — how far those steps actually moved you.

Two different questions.

"What did I do today?" and "Where is all of this taking me?" — MEAT Count answers the first, Goal Progress answers the second.

Supportive, never a ranking.
