THE MHG STANDARD

EVERYBODY EARNS.

Anybody can get on each one of our apps and earn — guests, floor, drivers, creators, owners. The standard is the same everywhere: FIRST on your first confirmed action · RETURN on your second · REGULAR from your third on. Nobody is left out, and nobody faces rejection: you get on, you build, and you earn the standard way, the proper way, with everybody else.

We run on strict guidelines and a code of conduct: real people, real listings, honestly confirmed actions, and your story told in your own words. That’s the deal — on every app we make.

— HOLDEN
ACROSS EVERY MHG APP

THE MHG CODE OF CONDUCT

Everybody earns. Nobody is left out. And we’re not playing around.

This code covers everybody on every MHG app — guests, floor, drivers, creators, owners, founders. One code, every door.

1. Earn it for real.

Standing and stamps come from confirmed actions only — FIRST on your first, RETURN on your second, REGULAR from your third. Fake orders, fake bookings, fake redemptions, or gaming the system in any form ends your earning. If it didn’t really happen, it doesn’t count.

2. Be who you say you are.

Real people, real listings, real stories — in your own words. No fake profiles, no invented reviews, no running a venue or an account that isn’t yours to run.

3. Respect the door.

Nobody gets messaged cold. Contact opens from both sides — you message when they accept. What somebody sets as private stays private. Harassment, of anybody, in any app, is an instant removal.

4. Money stays honest.

Prices, fees, and rewards are stated plainly and honored as stated. Founders raising on Meathos: itemized budgets, an update every 14 days, and backers’ money handled exactly as promised.

5. Everybody gets on.

Nobody faces rejection for credentials, titles, or follower counts. You get on, you build, and you earn the standard way, the proper way, with everybody else — and this code protects that the same way it protects everything else here.

Enforcement.

Break the code: first a warning, then your standing freezes, then you’re removed. Fraud skips the warning. Removed for fraud on one MHG app means removed across the family. Our call is final.

That’s the code. It doesn’t bend — and it applies to us too.
— HOLDEN
