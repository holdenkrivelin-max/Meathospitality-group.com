About

WHAT BECK IS

Beck is the personal AI inside Meat Life. Not an assistant that runs your day — a partner that keeps you on your intentions.

It knows what matters to you. It reminds you who matters to you. And when you're drifting, it gives you the push.

Most AI wants to decide for you. Beck is built on the opposite belief: the human makes the call. Beck suggests, Beck nudges, Beck holds you accountable — you stay in charge of your own life.

The name

HOW BECK GOT ITS NAME

Naming it took a whole night. The AI needed a human name — something that felt like a person, not a product.

We ran through thirty names. Every single one already belonged to someone else's AI. Taken, taken, taken.

Then Beck. Short. Human. Clean — nobody in AI had claimed it.

And it was sitting there the whole time: at your beck and call.

That's exactly what this is. Completely yours. On call. Whenever you need the push.
