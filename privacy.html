Privacy

MHG Group is the public brand of Meat Hospitality Group LLC, a small software company based in New York, NY. This page explains, in plain terms, what we collect on our websites and apps, how we use it, and how to reach us with questions. Last updated: October 28, 2026.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to run the sites and apps and to keep in touch with people who ask us to:

  • Account information you provide — for example, your name and email address when you join a waitlist, request early access, or contact us.
  • Content you choose to share — such as information you enter in an MHG app, where that app is available.
  • Usage analytics — basic, aggregated information about how the sites are used, such as pages visited, approximate location from an IP address, device and browser type, and referring pages.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, sensitive information such as passwords for other services, payment card numbers, or government identification numbers through these pages.

How we use it

We use the information above to operate the sites and apps, to improve them, to respond to messages, and to send launch and product updates to people who have asked for them. You can ask to stop receiving those updates at any time by replying to the message or contacting us.

We do not sell your personal data

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it for advertising to third parties, and we do not use the content of your private app activity to target ads.

Cookies and analytics

Our sites may use cookies and similar tools — small files stored by your browser — and analytics tools to understand which pages are visited and to keep the sites working. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect how some parts of a site work. Third-party tools we use, such as hosting, email, and analytics providers, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the sites and apps (for example, website hosting, email delivery, and analytics), and only as needed for that work. We may also share information if required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of MHG Group and its users.

Your choices and security

You can ask us to correct or delete the account information you provided, or to stop sending you updates, by contacting us at the address below. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or app can promise complete security, so please do not share highly sensitive information through these pages.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle personal information in an important way, we will update this page and change the date above. Continued use of the sites and apps after a change means you accept the updated page.

Contact us

Questions about this page can be sent to holdenkrivelin@meatapp.tech. Please write to: MHG Group (Meat Hospitality Group LLC), New York, NY.
